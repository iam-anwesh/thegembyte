Privacy policy

This policy explains what information TheGemByte collects and how we use it. Template — review with a legal advisor before launch.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your email address when you subscribe or reserve items, and anything you send via our contact form.
  • Stored on your device: your bag, wishlist and theme preference are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.
  • Analytics and affiliate cookies: we may use analytics to understand which pages are useful. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set cookies to attribute your purchase.

How we use it

To send the newsletter you signed up for, respond to your messages, fulfil orders for TheGemByte originals, and improve the site. We never sell your personal information.

Third parties

Retailers you visit through our links (such as Amazon Australia) have their own privacy policies. Payments for originals will be processed by a PCI-compliant payment provider; we never see or store your card details.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in every email, clear local storage from your browser settings, or contact us to request access to or deletion of your data.
